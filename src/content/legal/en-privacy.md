### 4. For What Purpose Will Iberosur Process User Personal Data and For How Long?

Depending on the User's requests, personal data collected will be processed for the following purposes:

- Manage requests for information and reservations, including payment management and preferences.
- Manage the newsletter subscription and subsequent delivery.
- Manage contact requests with Iberosur.
- Manage the sending of personalised commercial communications, where the User expressly consents.
- Manage the provision of requested services.
- Manage surveys and evaluations regarding the quality of Iberosur's services.

User data will be kept for the period necessary for each purpose or until the User requests withdrawal, opposes or revokes consent.

### 5. What User Data Will Iberosur Process?

IBEROSUR may process the following categories of data, depending on the request made by the User:

- **Identifying data:** name, surname
- **Contact information:** postal address, country of residence, mobile phone, email address

- **Personal characteristics:** date of birth, sex, nationality
- **Preference data**
- **Geolocation data**
